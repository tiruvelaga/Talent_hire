⚠️ Important

Once you start the exam, you cannot pause or exit. Ensure a stable internet connection and quiet environment before starting.

General Rules

  • This is an online exam. You must have a working computer with internet connectivity.
  • Read each question carefully before answering.
  • You can navigate between questions during the exam.
  • You can mark questions for review and come back to them later.
  • The exam timer will display remaining time. When time expires, answers are auto-submitted.
  • Do not attempt to cheat or use external resources during the exam.
  • All attempts are recorded and monitored.

System Requirements

  • Browser: Chrome (80+), Firefox (75+), Safari (12+), Edge (79+)
  • RAM: Minimum 2 GB (Recommended 4 GB)
  • Internet: Stable broadband connection (minimum 2 Mbps)
  • Screen: Minimum resolution 1024x768
  • Microphone: Required for system verification (if applicable)
  • Camera: May be required for identity verification

Do's

  • Read instructions carefully
  • Use a quiet, well-lit room
  • Close unnecessary applications
  • Enable full screen for better focus
  • Keep pen/paper for notes if allowed
  • Have water/coffee nearby
  • Test your mic before exam
  • Start 5 mins before scheduled time

Don'ts

  • Use mobile phones during exam
  • Refer to textbooks or guides
  • Take screenshots of questions
  • Share answers with others
  • Use proxy or automation tools
  • Minimize/switch tabs unnecessarily
  • Eat heavy foods right before
  • Attempt in a noisy environment

Scoring & Results

  • Marking Scheme: Each correct answer = 1 point. Wrong answer = 0 points.
  • Total Marks: 20 points (20 questions × 1 point)
  • Passing Score: Minimum 50% (10 out of 20 points)
  • Selection Criteria: Score ≥ 60% will be marked as "Selected"
  • Result Declaration: Results will be published within 2-3 business days
  • Answer Key: Official answer key will be released after all candidates complete the exam

Exam Duration & Timing

  • Total Duration: 10 minutes (600 seconds)
  • No additional time will be given after time expires
  • Timer shows remaining time in format MM:SS
  • You can submit before time expires
  • Early submission locks the exam permanently
  • System automatically submits answers when time expires

Malpractice & Disqualification

The following actions may lead to immediate disqualification:

  • Attempting to close or minimize the exam window
  • Using mobile phone or external devices
  • Seeking help from others or providing help
  • Using unfair means like looking at answer keys online
  • Taking screenshots or recording the exam
  • Switching tabs more than allowed times
  • Using browser extensions or suspicious tools
