Frequently Asked Questions

How do I login to the exam portal? +

Use the Registration ID and Password provided by the recruitment team. Default password is your Registration ID — change it from your profile after first login.

When will my exam results be published? +

Results are typically published within 2–3 business days. You will see a notification in your inbox the moment they are released.

What if my internet drops during the exam? +

Your answers are saved server-side. Reconnect within a few minutes and resume from where you left off. The timer continues running.

Can I retake the exam? +

Each exam has a maximum-attempts setting controlled by the admin. Check the exam card on your dashboard for the limit.

My result is held — what does that mean? +

The admin has temporarily withheld it. Raise a query with your Registration ID and the admin team will get back to you.
